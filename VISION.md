# VISION.md

**Authors:** S. Lisgo, Opus 4.7, Fable 5

## North star

A systematic markdown document flowdown that structures every stage of project definition, structure, and execution. Targeted at the team-of-one configuration: one human collaborating with many AI agents, not multi-person institutional teams. Project-type-agnostic: software, hardware (anything physical), social, or any combination. AI-optimized for efficient context reload across sessions; one deliberate prose exception (MOTIVATION.md) preserves shared human-AI ingestion.

## Guideposts

- **Team-of-one targeting.** One human + many AI agents, not multi-person institutional teams. Enables aggressive AI-optimization (no other human readers to please) and single-person sustainability framing in HUMAN.md.
- **Project-type-agnostic.** Same flowdown applies to software, hardware, social, and hybrid projects.
- **AI-optimized by default.** Files are written for an AI primary reader: directive voice, bullets, no filler. MOTIVATION.md is the prose exception, intended for shared human-AI reading.
- **Concision over explanation.** Files state what is, not what they are about. Reasoning lives in MOTIVATION.md (why) and DECISIONS.md (rationale), not embedded in specs.
- **HUMAN.md sits outside the flowdown.** Captures the human perspective on the collaboration and explicit human responsibilities. A reference doc in `reference/`, optional and not required for downstream projects; not referenced by INIT.md. Read by AI for workflow context, by humans as self-reminder.
- **Schema longevity is an experiment.** AI capability is advancing fast enough that this schema could be obsolete in months. The framework's survival, modification, or replacement under rapid AI advancement is itself the experiment's outcome.

## Objectives

Measurable project-level targets, derived from the guideposts. Expected to be met — the guidepost unrealizable license does not extend here.

- Every new portfolio project initialized per the INIT.md Entry Protocol, with CLAUDE.md bootstrap stub and recorded INIT.md version.
- Schema exercised by at least one project in each USE_CASES.md category (A–F).
- Master project index designed and delivered (post-stable-schema phase; open rows in DECISIONS.md).
- Schema-longevity outcome (survival, modification, replacement) tracked as DECISIONS.md entries and version-stamp history as AI capability shifts.

See MOTIVATION.md for the developed argument behind these entries.
