# Cold-Climate Heat Pump Field Study (Northern Utilities Consortium, 2025)

## 1. Scope
Fourteen ducted air-source heat pumps monitored in three Ontario towns, December 2024 – March 2025.

## 3. Results
### 3.1 Efficiency
Seasonal average coefficient of performance (COP) was 2.6. Below −20 °C, average COP fell to 1.4 (Table 3).

### 3.2 Backup heat
Electric resistance backup supplied 11 % of delivered heat over the season. Units sized to 100 % of design load used backup for 4 %; units sized to 70 % used backup for 19 % (Table 5).

## 4. Limitations
One winter; winter 2024–25 was 1.8 °C warmer than the 30-year normal.
